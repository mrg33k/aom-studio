One entry in the navigation set. `layout="tile"` is the wide desktop tools-row card; `layout="rail"` is the compact mobile side-rail icon. Active = accent fill (and, in rail, the square morphs from circle to rounded-square).

```jsx
<ToolTile icon={<HomeIcon/>} label="Home" active layout="tile" />
<ToolTile icon={<ChatIcon/>} label="Chat" layout="rail" />
```

The eight tools: Home, Chat, Organize, Review, Support, Tracker, Command, Live Scribe.
