The base surface every content block sits on. Frosted under the glass theme, flat under dark/light — same component.

```jsx
<Card pad={16}>
  <h3>Room files</h3>
  …
</Card>
```

Keep `glassy` on (default) unless you specifically want a non-blurring panel. Radius defaults to 16px (`--radius-card`); bump to `--radius-tile` (18px) for hero cards.
