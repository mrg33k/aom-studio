# Creative brief — /privacy (Corner privacy policy page)

frame class: type

## belief
People will trust an AI assistant with their business only if the company says plainly, in words a human wrote, what happens to their data. On a privacy policy, decoration reads as evasion — the words ARE the design.

## emotion
Calm.

## the one thing the user must be able to do
Understand what Corner collects and act on it: delete their account in-app or email hello@aom-inhouse.com (both linked in the Deleting-your-account and Contact sections).

## directions considered
1. Type-led legal document on the dark AOM support-desk canvas (CHOSEN — matches /support's calm dark surface; a policy is read, not browsed).
2. Iconography per section (rejected: icons on a legal text add noise, imply marketing).
3. Two-column FAQ layout (rejected: policies are quoted and linked by section; a single column keeps anchors and reading order simple).

## references
1. Apple privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy) — single column, type-led, generous line height.
2. Basecamp privacy policy — plain-language sections with bolded lead-ins.
3. Linear privacy page — dark canvas, quiet type, no ornament.
