# CV4 Redesign — Vision

CV4 is the clean evolution of CV3's dashboard. Pulls density from CV3 but loses the noise: single chat column, tasks live in the right drawer, mission/agent chips are the navigation primitives instead of colorful icons.

The component of record is `src/dashboard/CornerV4.jsx` and its `src/dashboard/cv4/` sub-modules. CornerV3 still exists in the tree but new feature work lands in cv4 unless explicitly stated otherwise.
