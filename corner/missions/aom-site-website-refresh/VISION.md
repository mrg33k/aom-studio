# Ahead of Market Website Refresh — Mission Vision

**Mission path:** `aom-site:website-refresh`

## North star

Make Ahead of Market feel unmistakably like the marketing department for companies that do not have one, with a portfolio-led homepage that is fast, legible, and confident on a phone.

## Scope

- Translate the approved Claude handoff into the existing React/Vite site.
- Make `/` the polished public homepage while preserving existing work, service, about, and contact routes.
- Reuse the current brief/contact flow and local portfolio assets where possible.
- Validate the production build and responsive behavior before release.
