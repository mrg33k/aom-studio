# Wolfpack Homepage Redesign Launch — Mission Vision

**Mission path:** `wolfpack:homepage-redesign-launch`

## What this mission is

The redesign and production cutover of Wolfpack Companies from its old GoDaddy site and AOM-hosted preview path to a polished, independent website at `wolfpackcompanies.com`.

## North star

Wolfpack has a fast, credible commercial-plumbing website that looks intentional on every screen, captures every lead, and can be updated without risking the AOM website.

## Pillars

- Carry the approved `Wolfpack Evolution B v2` design language through every page using the supplied page-specific comps.
- Preserve every current GoDaddy image before cutover and keep page-specific content and search intent intact.
- Make mobile behavior, accessibility, performance, navigation, and real lead delivery launch requirements rather than follow-up work.
- Host Wolfpack in a separate Vercel project and change GoDaddy DNS only after preview approval and rollback preparation.
