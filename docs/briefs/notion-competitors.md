## TL;DR
Notion dominates the all-in-one workspace category but faces pressure from focused tools. Obsidian wins on local-first/privacy, Coda on powerful databases, Linear on dev workflows.

## Company Overview
Notion Labs. Founded 2016. San Francisco. ~$10B valuation (2021). ~400 employees.

## Product
All-in-one workspace: notes, docs, databases, wikis, project management. Key differentiator: flexible block-based editor + relational databases.

## Pricing
- Free: limited blocks
- Plus: $8/month
- Business: $15/month
- Enterprise: custom

## Positioning
'The all-in-one workspace for your notes, tasks, wikis, and databases.' Targets knowledge workers and teams who need flexible structure.

## Key Competitors

| Competitor | Strength | Pricing |
|------------|----------|---------|
| Obsidian | Local-first, privacy, markdown | Free/$8/month |
| Coda | Powerful formulas, automations | Free/$10/month |
| Confluence | Enterprise, Atlassian ecosystem | $5.75/user/month |
| Linear | Dev workflow, speed | Free/$8/user/month |
| Roam Research | Bidirectional links, networked thought | $15/month |

## Weaknesses
- Performance issues at scale
- Mobile app is limited
- Offline mode unreliable
- Pricing jumps steeply for teams

## Gaps & Opportunities
- Notion users frequently complain about speed + reliability
- No strong AI-native alternative yet (Notion AI is a bolt-on)
- Privacy/local-first segment underserved by Notion
