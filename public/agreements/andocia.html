This Work-for-Hire Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date above by and between AOM In-House LLC ("Consultant," "AOM"), and Andocia ("Client"). Consultant and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

Client wishes to engage Consultant to design, build, and support a dedicated artificial-intelligence platform for Client's agency, and Consultant agrees to provide those services on the terms set out below.

1Scope of Work

Consultant will build Client its own AI platform, the same system Consultant runs Ahead of Market on, configured for how Client's agency operates.

  • Your own platform, on your own infrastructure. A dedicated instance built around Client's accounts, team, and workflow.
  • Custom tools for Client's accounts, with Client's whole team working in one system.
  • Onboarding of Client's first accounts and team, so the platform is doing real account work.
  • Client's way of working built in, so the system runs the way the agency already does.

2Kickoff & Timeline

The engagement begins with a two-day kickoff. In-person is recommended to move fastest (travel expenses billed separately and not included in the fee), and may be conducted virtually if Client prefers.

Following kickoff, Consultant will build Client's instance to an operational state within approximately four (4) weeks (roughly sixteen hours per week of dedicated build time), at which point the platform is live and running Client's first accounts.

3Support Term

For the thirteen (13) month term of this Agreement, Consultant will provide ongoing support of up to four (4) hours per week, plus phone availability, to keep the platform running and improving for Client.

4Fees & Payment

Total fee for the build and a full year of support: $117,000. A founding-partner rate. Travel for any in-person time is billed separately.

5Ownership

Upon Consultant's receipt of full payment, Client owns its instance and the deliverables, configurations, and custom code Consultant creates specifically for Client under this Agreement. That work product is Client's to keep.

Consultant retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing platform, underlying software, tools, frameworks, and general know-how, including any general improvements not specific to Client. Consultant grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use that underlying technology to the extent needed to operate Client's instance.

6Confidentiality

Each Party may receive confidential information of the other. Each Party will use the other's confidential information only to perform under this Agreement, will protect it with reasonable care, and will not disclose it to third parties without consent, except as required by law. This obligation survives termination.

7Independent Contractor

Consultant is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Client. Consultant controls the manner and means of performing the services, is responsible for its own taxes and expenses, and is not entitled to Client employee benefits.

8Term & Termination

This Agreement runs for thirteen (13) months from the Effective Date unless terminated earlier. Either Party may terminate for the other's material breach that remains uncured fifteen (15) days after written notice. The on-signing payment is earned upon execution; fees for work completed through the termination date are non-refundable. Sections 5, 6, 9, and 10 survive termination.

9Warranty & Liability

Consultant will perform the services in a professional and workmanlike manner. Except as stated, the services are provided "as is" without other warranties. Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, and each Party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the total fees paid by Client.

10General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona. It is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject and may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent.
