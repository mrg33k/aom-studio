01 The Project

Client is hosting two events, one in Los Angeles and one in New York. AOM brings the crew, the cameras, and the interview setup, and turns each event into a set of finished videos the team can publish the same week.

  • Crew — two camera operators: Anthony P. Matheson and Ash, AOM’s second operator
  • Cameras — three cinema cameras: two operated on the floor, one locked off wide on the stage
  • Interviews — a dedicated interview setup with lights and wireless microphones, ready anywhere in the venue
  • Sound — a direct feed from the house audio board plus room microphones, monitored all day
  • Coverage — one full event day per city, on site before doors open through the closing session
  • Deliverables — per city: a recap film, interview clips, a next-day teaser, and the full session recordings

Both cities get the same crew and the same deliverable set. Work beyond the list above is added by written change order at the rates in Section 07.

Dates, venues, the run of show, and the interview list are confirmed in writing (email is fine) at least fourteen (14) days before each event. One event day per city is included; additional days are added under Section 07.

03 Roles

AOM leads production on site — cameras, lighting, sound, and interviews — and owns post-production: editing, color, titles, sound mix, and music.

Client provides the run of show and the interview wish list before each event, one point person on site who can bring speakers and guests to the interview spot, crew credentials and load-in access at each venue, a feed from the house audio board where one exists, and on-camera consent from the people interviewed. AOM brings a simple release form for the day.

04 Crew & Equipment

AOM supplies all filming equipment for both events, including:

  • Cameras — three cinema cameras with lenses, tripods, and a gimbal for roaming coverage
  • Interview kit — key and fill lighting that sets up in a hallway, lounge, or in front of a step-and-repeat
  • Sound — wireless lavalier sets, a boom microphone, a board feed where available, and a backup recorder
  • Storage — media cards and on-site backup of all footage before the crew leaves the venue

Footage is backed up on site each day and kept by AOM for at least ninety (90) days after final delivery.

05 What You Get, Per City

  • Recap film — one (1) edited highlight video, 60 to 90 seconds, horizontal for web plus a vertical cut for social
  • Interview clips — up to six (6) on-camera interviews with speakers, sponsors, or guests of Client’s choosing, each edited into a 30 to 60 second clip, horizontal and vertical
  • Next-day teaser — a 30-second highlight delivered within twenty-four (24) hours of the event closing, so the team can post while attendees are still talking about it
  • Session archive — every main-stage session recorded on the locked-off camera with board audio, delivered as clean full-length files
  • Finishing — titles, color grade, sound mix, and licensed music on the recap film and teaser
  • Files — high-resolution masters (up to 4K), with vertical crops where noted

06 Travel

Travel for the two-person crew, Phoenix to Los Angeles and Phoenix to New York, is reimbursed to AOM at cost, on top of the fee, or booked directly by Client. AOM sends a travel estimate for approval before anything is booked. This includes:

  • Round-trip airfare for two, PHX to LAX and PHX to JFK or EWR, including checked-bag and oversize fees for camera and lighting cases.
  • Lodging for two, the night before and the night of each event day.
  • Ground transportation between airport, hotel, and venue, and airport parking in Phoenix.
  • Meals on travel and event days.

Flights are planned to land the day before each event so the crew can walk the venue and set up before doors.

08 Schedule, Review & Delivery

  • The next-day teaser is delivered within twenty-four (24) hours of each event closing.
  • The session archive is delivered within five (5) business days of each event.
  • Interview clips are delivered within seven (7) business days of each event.
  • The first cut of each recap film is delivered within seven (7) business days; the final within ten (10) business days of the event.
  • Two (2) rounds of revisions are included on each recap film and one (1) on the clips and teaser. A round is one consolidated set of notes from Client.
  • Changes beyond those rounds, or changes to direction already approved, are billed at $100 per hour with an estimate given before the work starts.
  • Feedback within two (2) business days of each cut keeps these dates. If feedback runs late, delivery moves by the same amount.

09 Rescheduling & Cancellation

  • Rescheduling an event with more than fourteen (14) days notice is free; that event’s share of the deposit moves to the new date.
  • Cancelling or rescheduling an event within fourteen (14) days keeps that event’s share of the deposit ($3,000) as a cancellation fee. Travel already booked stays Client’s cost.
  • If AOM cannot make an event for reasons within its control, that event’s share of the deposit is refunded or moved to a new date, Client’s choice.
  • Travel disruption outside either party’s control (weather, airline cancellation) is rescheduled in good faith with no penalty to either side.

10 Ownership & Usage

Once paid in full, the final videos may be used anywhere, for any purpose, with no time limit, and Client may share them with speakers, sponsors, and partners. Music is licensed for that use. Raw footage and the session archive stay with AOM beyond the ninety-day hold unless Client requests a full copy, available for an added fee. AOM may show the work in its portfolio after each event has taken place, unless Client asks in writing to keep it private.

11 The Standard Terms

AOM is an independent contractor. Each party keeps the other’s non-public information confidential, including anything shared on camera before it is published. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, and AOM’s total liability is limited to the fees paid under this agreement.
