Event Production Agreement

Two cities, two nights,
one story to carry forward.

This agreement covers AOM’s video, photo, livestream, and content work for the Hear the People Symposium in Los Angeles and New York City: capturing both round tables, streaming them live, and turning them into a recap film and a library of short clips for the campaign.

01 The Events

  • Los Angeles — Tuesday, November 17, 2026, venue confirmed by Client
  • New York City — Thursday, November 19, 2026, venue confirmed by Client
  • Format — moderated round table, plus audience arrival and guest interviews
  • Coverage per event — one event day: setup, doors, full program, and interviews after

Venue addresses, run-of-show, and call times are confirmed in writing (email is fine) at least fourteen (14) days before each event.

02 What AOM Delivers

  • Event video — multi-camera coverage of the full round table at both events
  • Guest interviews — short on-site interviews with attendees and speakers
  • Photography — event stills at both events, edited selects delivered in full resolution
  • Livestream — live multi-camera stream of each round table to Client’s chosen platform
  • Same-day social — phone-first vertical clips (TikTok, Reels, Stories) delivered during and right after each event
  • Recap film — one (1) recap video, 2–3 minutes, combining both cities
  • Clip library — up to fifteen (15) short vertical clips for social through the campaign window
  • Full program — the complete recording of each round table, lightly cleaned up, for long-form use

Stage graphics, lower-thirds, titles, and brand assets are supplied by Client or Market MindShift and dropped into the edits by AOM. Work beyond this list is added by written change order.

03 Crew & Equipment

AOM staffs and equips each event with a two-person crew: a lead director and camera operator (Anthony P. Matheson), and a second operator covering the second camera, stills, and the livestream. AOM supplies all cameras, lenses, lighting, wireless and board audio capture, streaming hardware, and media storage, with every card backed up on site before the crew leaves. AOM may use vetted local crew in either city at no change to the fee.

04 What Client Brings

  • Venue access for load-in at least three (3) hours before doors, plus a table and power near the stage.
  • A dedicated wired internet line at each venue with at least 20 Mbps upload for the livestream, and admin access to the streaming platform. If the venue cannot provide this, AOM will stream over cellular bonding as best effort and record in full either way.
  • Filming consent: registration and on-site signage tell attendees the event is recorded and streamed. Client collects any signed releases it needs from speakers and interviewees.
  • One point of contact for approvals (Client or Market MindShift) and feedback within three (3) business days of each cut.

06 Schedule & Revisions

  • Same-day social: during and within four (4) hours after each event.
  • Photo selects: within three (3) business days of each event.
  • Recap film: first cut by November 24, 2026, final within two (2) business days of notes, supporting the one-week post-event push.
  • Clip library and full-program files: by December 4, 2026.
  • Two (2) rounds of revisions on the recap film and one (1) on the clip library. A round is one consolidated set of notes. Extra changes are billed at $100 per hour with an estimate first.

07 Rescheduling & Cancellation

  • Moving an event with more than fourteen (14) days’ notice is free; the deposit carries to the new date, subject to crew availability.
  • Cancelling or moving an event within fourteen (14) days keeps that event’s share of the deposit ($2,000 per event) as a cancellation fee. Travel already booked with Client’s approval stays Client’s cost.
  • If AOM cannot cover an event for reasons within its control, that event’s share of the deposit is refunded in full.
  • Disruption outside either party’s control (weather, airline cancellation, venue closure) is resolved in good faith with no penalty to either side.

08 Ownership & Usage

Once paid in full, Client owns the final deliverables and may use them anywhere, for any purpose, with no time limit, including sharing them with Market MindShift for campaign use. Music in the edits is licensed for that use. Raw footage stays with AOM and is available on request for an added fee. AOM may show the work in its portfolio after the events are public, unless Client asks in writing to keep it private.
