01

Where AMG is today

AMG's software has two front doors:

  • The Portal is a large back office for AMG staff, with around 60 screens. It covers client records, programs, tasks, documents, approvals, and reports. It works in places, but it is cluttered, full of test entries, and hasn't been maintained since spring.
  • The OS is newer and closer to what AMG actually sells. It gives each family a safety score, watches world news for threats, plans trips and briefings, and uses AI to draft updates that a person approves. It looks premium, but it raises far too many false alarms to put in front of a family today.

The two don't share information. Staff juggle two logins, and families would see two different products.

Where we're going

One app. One login. One look. The screens each person sees depend on who they are.

Who logs inWhat they see first
The familyTheir safety score, the three things that matter this quarter, what's coming up on their calendar, and who at AMG is handling each item. Nothing technical and no noise. It answers "what should I worry about, and who's on it?"

The four phases

About 5 weeks · $15,000 fixed

Phase 1: Clean Up and Design (this agreement)

  • We take the keys to both apps and all their accounts.
  • We clear the test clutter, fix the broken screens, and stop the dashboard from showing false red.
  • The false alarms get quiet, so only news about the family is marked urgent.
  • Family documents move to permanent, encrypted storage.
  • We walk every screen with your team and decide what to keep, cut, or combine.
  • We deliver a clickable design of the One App.
  • We deliver a fixed price for Phase 2.

What you walk away with: two working apps that look clean and honest, plus the blueprint for the one app.

The four phases continued

About 8 weeks · est. $30,000–$50,000

Phase 2: Build the One App

  • One web address and one login for families, staff, and partners.
  • One consistent look, built from the design approved in Phase 1.
  • The family's view built first, because that is what AMG sells.
  • The best parts of the old Portal brought across: partner management, assessments, documents, approvals, and compliance checks.
  • Screens nobody uses are retired.
  • A weekly show-and-tell, so you see each screen before it's finished.

The price becomes fixed at the end of Phase 1. The range depends on how much of the old Portal AMG wants to keep.

About 4 weeks · est. $10,000–$20,000

Phase 3: Launch with a Real Family

  • Sample data is removed and real AMG staff are set up.
  • Every account gets two-step sign-in. This matters for this kind of client.
  • An outside security check, backups, and an alert to us if the app ever goes down.
  • AMG's own web address. No more "railway.app" links.
  • The first family is onboarded with AMG and AOM in the room.

What you walk away with: a live app a real family logs into and trusts.

Ongoing · $2,500–$5,000 / month

Phase 4: Care Plan

  • We keep it running: hosting, updates, the news feeds, and the AI writer.
  • We watch for problems and fix them.
  • A monthly block of hours for improvements and new ideas.
  • A monthly one-page update in plain language.

The tier depends on how many families are on the app and how much new work AMG wants each month.

How we work

  • AMG owns everything. We build what AMG asks for. The apps, the code, the data, and the accounts all belong to AMG.
  • You see everything before it ships. We hold a weekly 30-minute show-and-tell of the actual screens. We don't send status reports full of jargon.
  • One point person on each side. Patrik for AOM. AMG names theirs.
  • Plain language, always. We describe the work in terms of what's on screen and what the family experiences.
  • No surprises on price. Anything outside the agreed scope is written up and priced before we touch it.
