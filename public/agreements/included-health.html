Overview

AOM In-House will execute a comprehensive post-production workflow across four (4) distinct video assets for Included Health's SME and Interview video series. The scope covers all foundational phases of post-production, from color correction through final packaged handoff, establishing a seamless pipeline for client-side final mastering and delivery.

After Effects compositions and editorial outlines will be provided by Included Health. AOM will not be responsible for motion graphics execution or final delivery mastering.

Deliverables Per Video

Each of the four videos will receive:

  1. Color Correction — Uniform color treatment across all footage
  2. Base Edit — Narrative assembly based on provided editorial outline
  3. Round 1 (R1) Review Cut — Organized, labeled sequence for client review
  4. Round 2 (R2) Revision Cut — Revised cut incorporating R1 feedback
  5. Packaged Handoff — Optimized project files, master timelines, asset packages
