Try This Recipe

Overnight Oat Kefir Parfait

5 min prep + overnight · Serves 1 · Prebiotic + Probiotic
Ingredients
  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • ½ cup plain kefir (cow's or coconut)
  • 2 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp raw honey
  • ½ tsp pure vanilla extract
  • Handful of mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds or walnuts
Method
  1. Combine oats, kefir, chia seeds, honey, and vanilla in a jar. Stir well.
  2. Seal and refrigerate overnight (or at least 6 hours).
  3. In the morning, stir the mixture — it will be thick and creamy.
  4. Top with berries and seeds just before eating.
  5. Add a splash more kefir if you prefer a looser texture.
Oats are one of the richest sources of beta-glucan, a prebiotic fibre that feeds your beneficial gut bacteria. Kefir provides live probiotic cultures that replenish them. Together, this breakfast actively works on both sides of the microbiome equation.

Try This Recipe

Ginger Turmeric Bone Broth

15 min active · Serves 2 · Anti-inflammatory · Gut-lining support
Ingredients
  • 2 cups quality bone broth (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
  • ½ tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • Pinch of black pepper
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • Sea salt to taste
  • Optional: squeeze of lemon + fresh parsley
Method
  1. Warm bone broth in a small saucepan over medium-low heat — don't boil.
  2. Add ginger, turmeric, garlic, and black pepper. Stir to combine.
  3. Simmer gently for 5–8 minutes to infuse the flavours.
  4. Add apple cider vinegar and taste for seasoning.
  5. Strain if you prefer a clear broth, or drink as-is. Finish with lemon and parsley.
Bone broth is rich in collagen, glutamine, and glycine — nutrients that directly support the integrity of the gut lining. The black pepper here is essential: it activates turmeric's anti-inflammatory compound curcumin. Sip this slowly as a warm drink or use as a base for soups.
