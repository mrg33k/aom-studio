2Day 2 — Tue · Animated reel · The Shift
Three numbers
decide your reach.

60-75s animated reel, kinetic type, AOM motion design.
Must work muted — 70%+ watch with captions on.
LinkedIn: text-post version, no links.

0-3s — hook
"Nobody's seeing your posts.
Here's the actual reason."
3-25s
Instagram ranks every post on three numbers. Not followers.
Not likes. One: watch time — scroll past in a second? Buried.
(Three empty slots animate in; timer fills slot one.)
25-52s
Two: sends per reach — counts 3-5x more than a like.
(Paper-plane multiplies.) Three: likes per reach.
Still counted. Just last, and least. (Small heart.)
52-75s — payoff + closer
"Before you post, ask one question: would anyone SEND
this to someone? If no — that's why nobody's seeing it."
Closer: "We break down a shift like this every week. Follow."
Caption
Three numbers decide your reach in 2026. Most businesses
optimize for the one that matters least.
Save this. Send it to someone still buying likes.

Test hook variant first: "Your posts aren't bad. Your metrics are outdated."

3Day 3 — Thu · Animated reel · The Shift
The carousel
formula.

90s animated reel — a carousel builds itself slide-by-slide
as the VO runs. The reel practices its own advice.
LinkedIn: text post with the formula spelled out.

0-3s — hook
"Carousels still have the highest engagement rate
on Instagram. Here's the formula the winners use."
3-30s
Slide one has ONE job: stop the scroll. A specific promise,
not your logo. Then: one idea per slide — if it needs two
sentences, it's two slides. People swipe on rhythm.
30-60s
Mix media: a 5s video mid-carousel resets attention.
Second-to-last slide is the payoff — the checklist that gets
SAVED. Saves tell the algorithm you're worth showing strangers.
60-90s — the ask + closer
"Last slide: ONE ask. 'Send this to someone who needs it'
beats like-comment-share-follow combined."
Closer: "That's the formula. Watch us run it every week."
Caption
The carousel formula, in 90 seconds: 1 promise, 1 idea per
slide, motion in the middle, a save-worthy payoff, a single ask.
Try it on your next post and watch the saves column.

8-slide mixed-media carousel. One real AOM before/after —

a profile, or a single post. Closer: "Real work every Friday.
Send this to a business whose site still looks like slide 2."

Before and after, one day apart. Fridays are proof days:
real decisions, explained so you can steal the thinking.
