Matthew McDonald

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Matthew McDonald is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of K.R.A.K.E.N. Corps, a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) headquartered in Glendale, Arizona. He leads a portfolio of ventures built on a single principle: military-grade precision applied to real-world innovation.

His leadership philosophy was forged over more than eight years in the U.S. Navy Submarine Force, where he served as a Quality Assurance Officer responsible for multiple mission-critical systems. He later brought that same technical expertise to Raytheon as a Land Based Phalanx Weapons System Maintainer, supporting advanced defense equipment and training in high-pressure settings.

Beyond his executive roles, Matthew is a doctoral student in Psychology at Grand Canyon University. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Argosy University, and a Masters of Counseling from GCU.
