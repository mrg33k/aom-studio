Platypus Psychological Engineering

501(C)(3) NONPROFIT

Platypus Psychological Engineering is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to researching and developing new psychological theories for veterans and their families, neurodivergent individuals, and others with unique psychological needs. We advance frameworks that address gaps in current understanding, prioritizing evidence-based inquiry and practical application.

Our mission is to expand the science of mental health for the communities too often overlooked.
