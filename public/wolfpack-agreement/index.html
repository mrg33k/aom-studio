01 Scope — Your Marketing Department

AOM acts as Wolfpack’s outsourced marketing department. We own the strategy and the day-to-day execution across the channels below, so Wolfpack does not have to build the function in-house. Our focus is getting Wolfpack recommended to the buyers that matter — not chasing followers.

  • Strategy & direction — positioning, monthly plan, reporting
  • Website — rebuild, hosting oversight, ongoing updates
  • Video — production and editing for social and web
  • Photography & imagery — shoots and brand image
  • Organic content — LinkedIn-led, published on cadence
  • Email newsletter — build, write, and send
  • Google Ads & paid media — setup, management, optimization
  • Reputation — reviews, profiles, and search presence

Measured against KPIs. We set clear performance targets with Wolfpack at the start of the engagement and track them throughout the relationship. These KPIs are agreed together in the first planning session and reviewed in monthly reporting, so you always see what the work is producing and how it is trending.

Phase 01 is the website rebuild as the fast, visible win. From there the recommendation engine ramps over the following months. Specific deliverables and cadence are set in the monthly plan and may be adjusted by mutual agreement without amending this contract.

02 Term

This agreement begins on the Effective Date and runs for an initial term of three (3) months, giving the strategy the runway it needs to compound. After the initial term it continues month-to-month until either party ends it under Section 06.

04 What Each Side Brings

Wolfpack provides: timely access to accounts and assets, a single point of contact for approvals, and reasonable turnaround on reviews so work stays on schedule.

AOM provides: the people, tools, and management of the marketing function described in Section 01, and clear monthly reporting on what was done and what it produced.

05 Ownership

All final deliverables — website, videos, photos, and content — belong to Wolfpack once the invoice covering them is paid in full. Wolfpack owns what it pays for. AOM keeps the right to show the work in its own portfolio and case studies.

06 Ending the Agreement

After the initial three-month term, either party may end this agreement with thirty (30) days’ written notice. Wolfpack is responsible for fees through the end of the notice period; any work already delivered and paid for remains Wolfpack’s. AOM will hand off access and final files in an organized way.

07 The Standard Terms

AOM is an independent contractor, not an employee of Wolfpack. Each party keeps the other’s non-public information confidential. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, and AOM’s total liability is limited to fees paid in the prior three months. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona and is the entire agreement between the parties; changes must be in writing and signed by both.
