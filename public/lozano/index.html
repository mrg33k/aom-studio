Our family, serving yours

Built by one family, since 1928.

It began with Gumercindo and Hermina Santana Lozano, who came to Arizona from Mexico and built something meant to outlast them — a place where Northern Arizona families could be cared for with dignity, no matter who they were.

Today their grandson Richard carries it on. A U.S. Navy veteran with more than thirty years in the profession, he became sole owner in 2017 and added the crematory, so those in his care never leave the family's hands. The obituaries we keep reflect this whole region — every faith, every background, every life worth remembering.

Why a funeral matters

The quiet work a goodbye does.

i.Music

Music helps us feel

It helps everyone gathered to reach, assess, and begin to process the feelings of grief and loss that words alone can’t hold.

ii.Readings

Readings let us face it

The right words — sacred or simple, even humorous — help people acknowledge the reality of a death and gently face their pain together.

iii.Symbols

Symbols speak for us

When words fall short, flowers stand for love and beauty, food for nurturing and support, and candles for the spirit and its continuation.

iv.Memories

Memories carry them on

Sharing memories preserves the legacy of the one who died, and allows them to continue to live on in all of us who loved them.

v.Together

Gathering holds us up

A service brings together people who care for one another in an atmosphere of love and support, and helps us begin to truly acknowledge the loss.

Made personal

Woven from the life they lived.

Families reflect on a loved one’s hobbies, interests, accomplishments, and cherished memories — then we weave those details into the service through readings, displays, music, and shared stories.

Photo collages and slideshows, tribute videos, artwork, live or recorded music, keepsakes to carry home. Whether you choose burial or cremation — direct, memorial, or a full traditional service — it is shaped around the person, never a template.
