Overview

What We're Shooting

Event: 100th Higher Orbits Go For Launch! event, returning to Deerfield, IL — the same high school where the first event was held in 2016. Dorothy Metcalf-Lindenburger (STS-131) attending, as she was at the inaugural event. Mid-to-late June 2026 (exact date TBD — flag with Michelle, affects flight booking).

Crew: 3 people on the ground in Chicago. 2 camera operators + 1 producer/director (one cam doubles as director). In-house AOM team. Full coverage footprint — 2 cameras running simultaneously throughout, dedicated producing/directing, dedicated stills.

How the tiers work: Tier 1 is the entry offer — the Chicago trip and everything that ships from it. Tiers 2 and 3 are opt-in additions, priced per trip, that grow the production into a real series over time. The ask today is Tier 1. Tiers 2 and 3 are how we keep building together.

The math is honest. The $28,000 flat price reflects the expanded deliverable set vs. the original scope: 8–10 social cuts (vs. 4–6), 100 edited stills (vs. 30–50), raw footage archive delivery, and sponsor recognition package. The 7 additional person-days of post-production labor plus 15% contingency account for the $6,000 increase from the $22,000 floor.

What 3 people on the ground actually buys: 2 cameras running simultaneously throughout the event (no missed moments), a dedicated producer/director calling the shots without also operating a camera, dedicated stills coverage. The June event is a once-in-a-decade moment. 2-person "doubled roles" coverage is a risk.
