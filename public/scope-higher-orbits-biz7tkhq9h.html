01

What We're Scoping

AOM sends a 3-person crew (Phoenix-based) to Deerfield, IL for the 100th Higher Orbits Go For Launch! event. The shoot anchors a hero deliverable — a 5–to-10 min TV-quality piece — while the same footage powers four additional deliverables. Pre-production includes 2–3 remote interview shoots (travel-to-subject) to gather alumni and mentor voices before the Chicago trip.

In scope

  • Hero piece (5–10 min TV episode)
  • 2-day-turnaround recap (60–90 sec), delivered within 48 hours of the event
  • Social cuts pack (4–6 short verticals)
  • Hero photo set (30–50 edited stills)
  • Optional pre-event teaser (~30 sec) — priced separately below
  • 2–3 remote interview shoots (pre-event, travel-to-subject)

Out of scope

  • Full series episodes beyond the Chicago kickoff hero piece
  • Client PR / distribution strategy (could be an add-on pitch)
  • Motion graphics / animation beyond basic title cards
  • Original music score (library licensing assumed — see §7)

Event date assumption: Mid-to-late June 2026. Exact date TBD — affects booking windows for travel.

Open Questions + Assumptions

Critical — Needs Answer Before M3 Pricing
01
Crew day rates. Day rates above are market estimates for documentary freelancers, not AOM's actual internal rates. If Patrik/Ash/team are the crew, allocation may differ significantly. Confirm: what are the day rates or cost allocation for each crew role?
02
Event date. Exact June 2026 date for the Deerfield 100th event is unknown. Affects flight booking windows, lodging availability, pricing.
03
Drone. Does AOM own a drone suitable for this shoot? If not, add the rental line.
Scope Clarifications
04
Remote interview locations. Who are the 2–3 subjects and where? Directly affects flight costs. Could be Chicago-based (fold into main trip) or different cities entirely.
05
Photo delivery. What format/resolution does Michelle expect? Any licensing requirements (nonprofit use, social, press kit)?
06
Is the teaser in scope? Decision needed on whether the ~30-sec pre-event teaser is a base deliverable or an add-on. Shifts timing slightly — needs interview footage early enough to build it a week before the event.
07
Crew composition. Is this Patrik + Ash + one more? Or all hires? Affects whether to frame costs as internal labor allocation or subcontractor rates.
Pricing Structure (for M3)
08
How to present pricing. Options: (a) all-in project fee, (b) production day rate + post, (c) milestone-based bundle. For a nonprofit, an all-in "production partnership" package likely feels cleanest and most fundable for Michelle (she can take it to sponsors/board).
09
Nonprofit rate consideration. Higher Orbits is a 501(c)(3). Offer a nonprofit discount, or position as full market price given scale, travel, and deliverable count? Recommend pricing at market and framing the investment as what it takes to tell the story right — not discounting.
10
Payment structure. Recommend milestone-based: 30% deposit to confirm and book travel, 40% at shoot wrap, 30% at final delivery. Three milestones keeps both sides protected.
