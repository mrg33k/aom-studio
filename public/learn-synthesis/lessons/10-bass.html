Module 10 · Build lab

Build a bass: tight, punchy low end

A great bass feels like a pillow hitting you in the chest. Stack a deep sine sub with a brighter saw layer, squeeze it with a low-pass filter, and snap it with a tight envelope. This is the shape of every bass sound you love.

The idea

The two-layer trick

Most basses are not one thing. They are two things stacked: a sub bass (very deep, round, like distant thunder) and a bright layer (higher, with snap and personality).

The sub is a warm sine wave. The bright layer is a sawtooth. When they play together and flow through a low-pass filter (which cuts all the harsh highs), your ear hears one smooth, punchy bass.

Add a tight, snappy amp envelope (attack fast, release fast) and the bass feels alive and present, like your fingers are hitting real strings. Slow the envelope and it becomes a pad. Tight envelope, two layers, one filter. That is bass.

On the keys: one keygroup with Key Track on, played across the low keys. Keep the track in Mono so notes don't overlap and the bassline stays tight.

On the MPC · Keygroup patch

What the finished patch looks like

Two layers on Layer 1 and Layer 2. Layer 1 is Warm Sine at a low pitch for sub bass. Layer 2 is Saw an octave or two higher for bite. Both flow into Filter 1, a low-pass, which rolls off the highs. The Amp Envelope is snappy: fast Attack, fast Release. That is the whole recipe.

Build it with me · six steps

Make a bass patch from scratch

  1. New keygroup trackSet the track type to Keygroup. This is where synthesis happens.
  2. Layer 1: Warm Sine subOn Samples/Oscs tab, set Layer 1 source to Oscillator, choose Warm Sine. This is your deep bass pillow.
  3. Layer 2: Saw for biteAdd a second layer, set it to Saw oscillator. Tune it up two octaves from the sine for brightness without muddiness.
  4. Add a low-pass filterOn the Filters tab, set Filter 1 to Low-pass, cutoff around 1.5 kHz. This smooths both layers into one warm tone.
  5. Make the envelope snappyOn Amp Envelope tab, set Attack fast (40 ms), Release fast (80 ms). This is what makes it punch instead of sounding mushy.
  6. Stack and balanceOn the Tune/Mix sub-tab, pull Layer 2 level down a notch (sine should be louder). Play a note. That is your bass.
