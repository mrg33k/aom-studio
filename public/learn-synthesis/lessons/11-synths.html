Module 11 · Build lab

Build a synth lead

A lead is a synth you play like a solo voice. By the end you'll have a bright, expressive synth with a snappy filter that moves, vibrato that bends the pitch, and the ability to play fast note-to-note without overlap. Then you'll hear it in real time on mini keys.

The idea

A lead is shaped attention

A lead is a synth voice you play one note at a time, like a saxophone. It has to cut through the mix, respond fast, and feel alive. The secret is three ingredients: a bright start, a moving middle, and a quick fade.

Bright start means a sawtooth oscillator (sharp, buzzy) flowing through a filter that snaps open and closed. When you hit a key, the filter envelope slams the cutoff up, then settles down. Pitch wobble (vibrato) adds human warmth and expression. Mono mode means only one note plays at a time, so new notes cut off the old ones, which is what a saxophone does too.

You build this from a sawwave, a low-pass filter with an envelope, an LFO for vibrato, and one switch to toggle each effect on and off so you hear what it adds.

Build it with me · the complete lead synth

Six steps to a responsive lead

  1. Start with Mono modeNew track, set it to Keygroup, then go to the GLOBAL tab and switch Poly to Mono. This way only one note plays at a time, and new notes cut off the old ones.
  2. Choose Saw for Layer 1On the Samples/Oscs tab, set Layer 1 to Oscillator, then pick Saw from the list. Sawtooth is bright and cuts through.
  3. Make it respond fastOn the Amp envelope, cut the Attack to zero (instant response) and set Decay to about 0.3 seconds. A lead has to feel snappy, not mushy.
  4. Add a filter with punchOn the Filters tab, set Filter 1 to low-pass, Cutoff to 100-110, and Resonance to 70. Now turn up Env Depth to 80 so the filter envelope opens it on each note attack.
  5. Wire vibratoOn the LFO 1 tab, set Rate to 4-5 Hz and turn up the Pitch amount to 5-8. In the Mod Matrix, route LFO 1 to Pitch. Now the sound wobbles like a horn player adding expression.
  6. Play it and tweakHold a note and listen. If it's too bright, lower Cutoff or Resonance. If the filter envelope is too much, lower Env Depth. If vibrato is annoying, cut the LFO level. A lead is about feel, not rules.
