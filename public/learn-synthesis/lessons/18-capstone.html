On the MPC · Your signal path

The whole journey

Every sound you make on the MPC takes this exact path. Press a key. Follow the signal all the way through. Nothing happens by magic.

Advanced Keygroup EngineSIGNAL PATH
  1. → You press a key (velocity, pitch)
  2. → Keygroup picks a layer (Sample or Oscillator)
  3. → Oscillator makes a shape (Sine / Saw / Wavetable / Noise / FM / etc.)
  4. → Modulators move knobs (Envelopes, LFOs, Velocity, Mod Wheel)
  5. → Amp Envelope times your volume (Attack / Hold / Decay / Release)
  6. → Filter Envelope shapes tone (Cutoff movement)
  7. → Two Filters (Parallel or Serial) color the sound
  8. → Amp out (Pan, Level, Mute)
  9. → You hear it

Now you can

The moment you have arrived at

  1. Choose an oscillator source.Sine, Saw, Square, or Wavetable. Each one is a different starting shape. No wrong choice.
  2. Dial in a cutoff.High = bright and piercing. Low = dark and warm. A good default is somewhere in the middle and then you move it.
  3. Set Attack and Release.Fast attack and release = stab or staccato. Slow attack and release = pad or held breath. The two together define the shape of time.
  4. Optional: LFO to Filter.Turns the cutoff into a slow wobble. Makes a pad feel alive. Makes a bass feel less static.
  5. Press keys. Hold them. Listen.Play a chord. Play a melody. Twist the knobs while notes are ringing and hear them move in real time.
  6. Name it.Every choice you made led here. You picked a tone, a filter color, a time shape, a movement. This is a patch. It is yours. It has a name.
