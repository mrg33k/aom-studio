FAQ

Do I need an Akai MPC to take this?

No. Every lesson makes sound right in your browser and shows the MPC screen recreated on the page, so you can learn the whole engine first. When you sit down at a real MPC, it will already feel familiar.

Do I need to know music theory?

No. We explain everything like you are brand new. Plain words first, the real name second, and a sound you can hear for every idea.

What will I be able to do at the end?

Build your own bass, 808s, pads, synth leads, piano, choir, horns, and RNB and hip hop sounds from scratch, and explain how every part of the engine works in your own words.

How long is it?

Nineteen short lessons. Part one teaches the machine, part two is a guided build of each sound, and it ends with a make-your-own capstone. Go at your own pace.

Is it really for beginners?

Yes. If you have never made a sound before, start at lesson one. If you already produce, jump straight to the build lab.
