QUESTIONS

The ones we hear most.

How long does it take to get started?

Usually about a week. We put together a plan, get access to your accounts, and schedule the first shoot. By the end of the first month, things are running.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No contract. Month to month. Everything we make is yours — the plan, the photos, the website, the ad account. You could leave tomorrow and keep all of it.

What if I only need one thing?

We do that too. A crew for a day, a website rebuild, or just somebody running your ads. You can start with one thing and add from there, or keep it to the one thing.

Who will I be working with?

The same person. You'll have one contact from the first call onward. They'll know your business, your preferences, your jobs. You're not starting over with someone new every few months.

How do you handle the filming?

We come to you. Wherever the work is happening — job site, office, warehouse, rooftop at 4:50 AM — we show up with a crew. Photos, drone, and video. We work around your schedule.

What industries do you work with?

Construction, trades, and mechanical are where we have the most depth. We also work with hospitality, wellness, nonprofits, and a few others. If the work happens in the field, we're comfortable there.
